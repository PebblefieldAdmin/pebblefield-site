Pebblefield Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Pebblefield is a puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: there are no accounts, we do not collect your name or contact details, and we never sell your information. Data only leaves your device to save your progress to your own iCloud, rank your Blitz scores on Game Center, confirm purchases, show the ads you choose to watch, and (if you leave it on) send anonymous usage statistics.

Who we are

Pebblefield is made by an independent developer. You can contact us at pebblefield@hotmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (your world, Glints, streaks, moods, achievements and settings) is saved on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iPhone backups. If you copy a backup code from the game, the code contains the same progress and goes wherever you choose to paste it.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Pebblefield saves a copy of your progress in your private iCloud storage so it can appear on your other Apple devices. This copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud account. We cannot see or access it. You can turn this off in the iPhone Settings app under your name, then iCloud.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your ranked Blitz of the Day scores are sent to Apple's Game Center, where other players can see them on the daily leaderboard next to your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We only see the rankings that Game Center shows to every player. You can sign out in the iPhone Settings app under Game Center; the game works without it.

Anonymous usage statistics

If you leave "Share anonymous usage" on (in the game's Settings), the game sends anonymous signals about which features are used, such as finishing the tutorial or watching an optional ad, to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU. They carry a random identifier created on your device, which TelemetryDeck hashes again, and never your name, Apple ID, location or device identifiers. They are not used for advertising or shared with anyone else. You can switch this off at any time in Settings. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. To confirm purchases and to know whether a Commuter Pass subscription is active, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipts from Apple along with a random identifier created for the app. It is not linked to your name or Apple ID. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Ads

Pebblefield only shows an ad when you choose to watch one for a bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect:

  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location;
  • how you interact with the ad;
  • your device's advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking when iPhone asks.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's menu under "Ad privacy choices". You can also turn off tracking for all apps in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Ads only use your advertising identifier if you allow tracking when iPhone asks.

Children

Pebblefield is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. Deleting the app removes the progress saved on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to the iPhone Settings app, then your name, iCloud, Manage Storage, Pebblefield. Turning off "Share anonymous usage" stops the usage statistics. For information held by Apple, Google, RevenueCat or TelemetryDeck, see their policies linked above, or contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
