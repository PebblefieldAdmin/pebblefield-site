Support

How do I restore my Commuter Pass?

Open the menu (the two lines at the top right) and tap Restore purchases. It is also at the bottom of the shop. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel my Commuter Pass?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pebblefield. Cancel at least 24 hours before the month ends to avoid the next charge. After you cancel, your pass keeps working until the end of the month you have already paid for.

How do I move my progress to a new iPhone?

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress follows you: open Pebblefield on the new iPhone and choose "Use the iCloud progress" when it asks. You can also open the menu, tap Back up progress, copy the code, and paste it into Restore on the new phone. Glint packs come with your progress, and a Commuter Pass comes back with Restore purchases.

How does the Blitz of the Day work?

Everyone plays the same map with the same twist for five minutes. Your first run each day is the ranked one; replays are practice. The top 5, and everyone in the top 10%, win Glints, paid the next time you open the game. A new Blitz starts every day at midnight Singapore time (16:00 UTC). Rankings use Game Center, so sign in to Game Center to appear on the leaderboard.

Why did part of the field lock?

Hitting a mine locks that sector. Clear all 8 sectors around it to reopen it for free, or reopen it straight away with Glints or a short ad.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it, the menu has an Ad privacy choices button. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in the Settings app under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
