About the game

In one line

Pebblefield is calm, no-guess Minesweeper where every tile you clear paints one pixel of a hidden 8-bit painting.

In a paragraph

Pebblefield turns Minesweeper into something quiet and rewarding. The field never ends: read the numbers, flag the mines, open the ground, and a hand-made pixel painting appears beneath your fingers, a torii gate at sunset, a caravan crossing the dunes, a turtle on the reef. There are 200 paintings to uncover across nine lands. Every field can be solved by logic alone, and if the numbers ever leave you stuck, a safe tile glows for free. When you want a challenge, the Blitz of the Day puts everyone on the same five-minute map, and Pebble Dash is classic Minesweeper against the clock.

Key features

  • Minesweeper that paintsEvery tile cleared is one pixel of a hidden painting. Zoom out to see what you have made.
  • 200 paintings, nine landsFrom the Zen Garden at the heart of every world out to the Northern Lights, Under the Sea, the Silk Road, the Rainforest and more.
  • No guessing, everEvery field is solvable by logic. Truly stuck? A safe tile lights up, free.
  • An endless fieldThe ground goes on forever. A mine only locks its own sector, so a world never ends.
  • Blitz of the DayOne map for everyone, five minutes, a new twist each day, a leaderboard and a podium for yesterday's top three.
  • Pebble DashClassic Minesweeper against the clock, Easy to Extreme, with global leaderboards.
  • Gold stars and timersPaint a picture with no mistakes for a gold star, and beat your time on it.
  • Make it yoursStyle sets, pebbles, flags, dig effects, cascades and celebrations. Looks only, never the rules.
  • Calm by designSoft sounds, gentle motion, colour-blind friendly numbers, day and night moods, play offline.
  • Fair and freeNo account needed. Ads only when you choose one, and an optional Commuter Pass to skip them all.
